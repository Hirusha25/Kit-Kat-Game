KitKat Hot Cocoa brand elements, extracted from "New Kit Kat Hot Cocoa Toolkit 2025" (Nestle Professional / VML, internal use only).
These are reference-quality crops and cutouts for UI design and prototyping. Request the vector masters from the Nestle Content Hub before production.

Colours
  KitKat Red   PANTONE 186  |  CMYK 10/100/90/0  |  RGB 186,0,24  |  HEX #BA0018
  White        RGB 255,255,255 (HOT COCOA text on dark placements)
  Cocoa brown  (from imagery, not an official swatch) approx #6B2E1F to #8C4A2F

Fonts
  Headlines: heavy condensed grotesque as used in the toolkit (Franklin Gothic Heavy family)
  "HOT COCOA" descriptor: Franklin Gothic Heavy, CAPS only, never changed
  "Got Hotter" steam text: Relation Two Bold (Adobe Fonts) / Daughter of Fortune (Content Hub), rendered as steam
  "break" in copy: hand-drawn KitKat "break" lettering from the Have a break lockup

Logo rules
  Always use the KitKat HOT COCOA logo (oval + HOT COCOA), not the plain KitKat oval on its own
  Do not recolour, do not change the font or case of HOT COCOA
  Clear space = height of the H in HOT COCOA on all sides
  Brand name in text: KITKAT(R) in capitals
  Legal line on all materials: "KitKat(R) Reg. trademark of Societe des Produits Nestle S.A."
  Tagline "Have a break, have a KitKat(R)" only on red, white lettering, never shortened or altered
