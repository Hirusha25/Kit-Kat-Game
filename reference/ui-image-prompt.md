# KitKat Hot Cocoa motion game: UI storyboard image prompt

Paste the prompt below into ChatGPT (image generation). Attach three references: the launch invitation image (red, gold ribbon, chocolate wave), plus `key-visual-portrait.png` and `kitkat-hot-cocoa-logo-red-on-white.png` from the brand folder. Ask for a 2:3 or 3:4 image so the nine portrait screens stay large.

---

Create a single high-resolution UI storyboard sheet showing nine portrait kiosk screens (each 9:16, like a 1080x1920 digital signage totem) laid out in a 3x3 grid on a neutral light grey background, with a small white label under each screen. This is a motion-controlled arcade game for a KitKat Hot Cocoa kiosk: the player holds a KitKat mug in front of a webcam and moves it left and right to catch falling KitKat wafer fingers in a glass mug of hot cocoa on screen.

Look and feel, follow strictly, matching the KitKat Hot Cocoa launch invitation: the background is deep KitKat red (#BA0018) with a warm radial glow in the centre fading to a darker crimson vignette at the edges, with a thin gold foil ribbon sweeping in from the top-left corner and tiny gold four-point sparkles scattered near the headlines. The bottom third of every screen is a photoreal wave of molten milk chocolate, glossy, with a splash crest and floating droplets, blending into the red above it, so the screens feel rich and celebratory. Headline style is two-tier like the invitation: a gold metallic serif in capitals (e.g. "A NEW CHAPTER") on top, and an elegant white calligraphic script beneath (e.g. "The Same Iconic Break"). Body copy is clean white sans-serif, with a champagne-gold highlight line for the warm message. Info rows use thin gold line icons (calendar, clock, pin style) with gold labels and bold white values, separated by thin gold vertical rules. Pills and badges are rounded with a thin gold outline on red. The primary button is a gold metallic gradient pill with dark red capital letters spaced out (like "RSVP"). The campaign line "Got Hotter" stays as white steam-script. The product logo is the red KitKat oval with "HOT COCOA" in white capitals beneath it, always shown as supplied, never recoloured, centred and large where it appears. The hero object is a clear glass mug of hot cocoa with a cocoa-dusted foam top and the KitKat HOT COCOA logo on the glass, photoreal, with wisps of steam. KitKat wafer fingers are photoreal milk chocolate fingers with the embossed KitKat name. Palette is only deep red, gold, white and chocolate brown with cream foam. Premium, glossy and festive, not flat. High contrast for a 55-inch screen viewed from two metres. No clutter, no stock photos of people.

The nine screens, in order:

1. ATTRACT: Gold ribbon top-left, "NEW" cup badge. Gold serif "A NEW CHAPTER" over white script "The Same Iconic Break", then the steam-script "Got Hotter". Centre: the hero glass mug with wafer fingers falling into it, sparkles around it. Below: a gold-outlined pill "Pick up the mug to play" with a small mug icon, and a QR code tile with a gold frame labelled "Scan to register". Chocolate wave along the bottom. Footer: Nestlé Professional logo small, legal line in tiny text.

2. REGISTER (tablet form, shown as a 4:3 tablet screen inside the portrait frame): Red background with the gold ribbon, a rounded card with a thin gold outline. Gold serif headline "REGISTER" over white script "for your break". Fields with gold line icons: Name, Mobile number, Email, a consent checkbox. Gold gradient button "GET MY CODE".

3. YOUR CODE: Red screen, white script "Your code", a gold-outlined card showing a six-character code in huge gold serif type, e.g. "BRK-4F7Q", a short white line "Enter this on the kiosk to start", and a thin gold 60-second expiry ring. Chocolate wave at the bottom.

4. ENTER CODE on the kiosk: Red screen, six gold-outlined code boxes at the top, a keypad of gold-outlined keys with white characters, a gold gradient "START" button. Small helper text "Got your code? Type it here".

5. SHOW YOUR MUG (calibration): Red screen with a live camera window in a rounded gold frame, a dotted gold outline of a mug in the centre of the window, the instruction in white script "Hold the KitKat mug up to the camera", and a gold progress ring filling as the mug is detected.

6. COUNTDOWN: Dim red screen, giant gold metallic serif "3" in the centre with steam and sparkles around it, the hero mug sitting on the chocolate wave at the bottom, the score pill and 45-second timer already visible at the top.

7. GAMEPLAY: Top bar: score on the left in a gold-outlined pill with a gold line icon, countdown timer on the right in a gold-outlined pill with a gold ring. Middle: six to eight KitKat wafer fingers falling at different heights, one gold-foil bonus finger, one dark melted chocolate blob decoy. Bottom third: the glass hot cocoa mug moving along a subtle gold guide line just above the chocolate wave, with a splash and a gold "+10" popup where a finger has just landed. A faint, blurred mirrored camera feed sits behind the game, 20 percent opacity, so the player sees themselves.

8. LOST TRACKING: Gameplay frozen and darkened, a gold-outlined card in the centre with a gold mug line icon and "Show the mug to the camera", a thin gold countdown bar beneath.

9. RESULT: Red screen with gold ribbon and sparkles, gold serif "YOUR BREAK" over white script "Got Hotter!", a gold-outlined score card with the final score in large gold serif, a tier label "Legend / Break / Snack", the player's first name, and a QR code in a gold frame to claim a voucher. Below: "Thanks for taking a break" and a gold gradient "PLAY AGAIN" button, chocolate wave along the bottom. Footer: legal line "KitKat® Reg. trademark of Société des Produits Nestlé S.A."

Render all nine screens with consistent spacing, type sizes and the same hero mug asset, as a polished UI design presentation. Sharp text, no misspellings, no watermark.
